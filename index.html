Why LOAD exists2 of 16

It started with a chat window.

In 2026 I trained for the European Championships in Almere as a GB age-grouper, this time without a coach. So I tried something different: I opened a general-purpose AI chat and made it my coach.

It was genuinely useful. It reasoned with me, challenged me, and answered at 10pm when I needed it. For the first time I had a view on my training, not just a plan.

For a while, it felt like the future of coaching.

What we believe12 of 16

How your coach behaves.

It shows its working

Every recommendation comes with the reasoning, so you can push back.

It will disagree with you

A coach that always says yes isn't coaching.

It knows its limits

Injury and health questions get pointed to a professional.
