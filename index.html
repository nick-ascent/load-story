Why LOAD exists2 of 13

It started with my own race.

In 2026 I trained for the European Championships in Almere as a GB age-grouper, without a coach. I had my Garmin, TrainingPeaks, and a long-running AI conversation that I fed with my sessions, my niggles and my work weeks.

It gave me what I wanted from a coach: personal, always there, and willing to reason things through with me. But I was holding it together by hand, re-explaining myself and copying data across.

A GB teammate had built the same workaround on his own.

Two athletes, same fix, no product. LOAD is that coach, built properly.

The problem3 of 13

Plans are everywhere. Judgment isn't.

Every training app sells you a plan: generated, adaptive, personalised, but still a plan you follow. When life gets in the way, you're on your own again.

What a coach does4 of 13

What you actually pay a coach for.

A view

Someone who looks at your week and tells you what they think, including "don't".

Your whole situation

The knee, the job, the kids, how you respond to threshold blocks. Context no device records.

A memory

They remember last winter, and what happened the last time you stacked three hard days.

The idea5 of 13

LOAD is a coach you talk to.

Not another plan. It reads your training, knows your context, and reasons with you, from today's session to race day and everything in between.

How it works7 of 13

Three steps to your coach.

  1. 1

    Create an Event Plan

    Set up a plan for your race, like "Almere 2027". Everything for that goal lives there.

  2. 2

    Bring in your training

    Upload your training files so the coach works from what you actually did.

  3. 3

    Talk to your coach

    Start a conversation, or keep named threads like Nutrition. Browser or phone.

In beta, training comes in by file upload. Direct Garmin, TrainingPeaks, Wahoo and Strava connections come next.
